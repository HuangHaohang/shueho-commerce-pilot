## Cross-Market Opportunity

The market consistently optimizes one of three things: broad external evidence, fast content generation, or strong single-platform execution. Public product materials rarely show all of the following in one system:

1. immutable enterprise Product/SPU/SKU facts;
2. claim-level evidence and limitations;
3. one persisted Agent conversation and decision memory;
4. explainable gates rather than one opaque AI score;
5. Product/claim/brand/channel QA on every artifact;
6. approval, exact-once side effects, readback and tenant isolation;
7. experiment results linked back to the exact hypothesis and creative variant.

Commerce Pilot should not copy competitor navigation or generic AI tools. Its durable advantage is the governed chain:

```text
Product revision
  -> Evidence ledger
  -> Explainable Scorecard / decision Gate
  -> Approved claims and experiments
  -> Campaign asset graph
  -> Four-layer creative QA
  -> Governed publish/readback
  -> Performance evidence for the next retrospective
```

## Implemented From This Review

- Product Insights now requires dimension-level Scorecards, proposed decision Gates and bounded validation experiments.
- Report receipts reconcile against same-Turn Harness tool activity and cannot self-assert verification.
- Creative Space adds `commerce-campaign-pack` and `commerce-creative-qa` native specialist Skills.
- Main/gallery media Turns require selected Product revisions and tenant-owned image artifacts before Harness execution.
- Deep creative-canvas reconciliation no longer silently truncates 240 sources or deletes projections when history is incomplete.

## Remaining Product Roadmap

- Governed first-party orders, traffic, conversion, advertising, returns, support, inventory movement, cost and contribution-margin evidence.
- Immutable Product Media and Brand/Channel specification revisions.
- Planned batch generation with quote/approval and per-cell idempotency.
- Governed asynchronous video render and publish tools with downstream readback.
- Experiment and performance receipts that connect a measured result to one exact Product/claim/creative variant.
